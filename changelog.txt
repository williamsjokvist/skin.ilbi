[B]0.0.1[/B]
• Initial commit based on Estuary.

[B]New home screen[/B]
• The side menu is now a centred nav bar across the top of the screen, with text labels
  and a search icon.
• The highlight behind the selected item slides across as you move between items, and
  rounds into a circle over search.
• Removed the clock bar and Kodi logo from the top of the home screen.

[B]Movies and TV shows only[/B]
• Music, live TV, radio, games, pictures, add-ons and weather have been removed
  throughout the skin.
• "TV shows" is now just "Shows".

[B]Kodi Options[/B]
• The power menu is now "Kodi Options" and includes Settings, so the separate settings
  button is gone. It opens from the Kodi logo in the top right.

[B]Darker, simpler look[/B]
• Black background everywhere, with the patterned backgrounds removed.
• One fixed colour scheme and one theme instead of a picker for each.
• New typeface throughout: Nunito.

[B]For developers[/B]
• Fonts are downloaded by a script rather than committed to the repo (mise task:
  install-fonts).
• scripts/mock-library.sh builds a sample movie/TV library for working on the skin.
